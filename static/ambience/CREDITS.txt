Ambience recordings in Pensieve

Each file was cut from the recording below, crossfaded into a seamless loop (the piano plays the
whole piece with a short pause), evened out in loudness, and saved as Ogg Opus. Every file has a
quarter second of its own sound before and after the loop, which the app doesn't play.

rain.ogg
  "Sirutėnai, Lithuania, rain on leaves" by alas23/sala, from radio aporee ::: maps.
  Public Domain Mark 1.0.
  https://archive.org/details/aporee_66409_76780
  Used: 2:45 to 3:49, looped as 60 seconds.

fire.ogg
  "944 fireplace hiss and crackling" by Piotrek Zyla, from radio aporee ::: maps.
  Public Domain Mark 1.0.
  https://archive.org/details/aporee_60408_69385
  Used: 0:07 to 1:10, looped as 60 seconds.

cafe.ogg
  "Coffee Shop at the Capucins" by Joseph Sardin, BigSoundBank.com.
  CC0 1.0 (public domain).
  https://bigsoundbank.com/coffee-shop-at-the-capucins-s2561.html
  Used: 0:08 to 1:27, looped as 75 seconds.

piano.ogg
  Erik Satie, Gymnopédie No. 1, played by Robin Alciatore, recorded for Musopen.
  Released into the public domain by the performer.
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Erik_Satie_-_gymnopedies_-_la_1_ere._lent_et_douloureux.ogg
  The whole piece, in mono, with a three-second pause before it repeats.
  With thanks to Musopen (musopen.org), as they ask.
