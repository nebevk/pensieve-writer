Keystroke sounds in Pensieve

Chosen by the owner on 8 October 2026 from Mechvibes sound packs (https://mechvibes.com), for Pensieve's
own, private use. Only the keys Pensieve plays were cut from each pack, evened out in loudness, and saved
back to back as one Ogg Opus file per style; clips.json says where each key's sound is.

typewriter/   Lincoln Typewriter (custom-sound-pack-1203000000091): keystroke, space, backspace, and the
              carriage return with its bell for Enter.
mechanical/   CherryMX Brown - ABS keycaps (sound-pack-1200000000005), a sound for each key.
soft/         EG Oreo (sound-pack-1200000000010), a sound for each key.
