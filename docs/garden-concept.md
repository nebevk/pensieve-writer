# The garden: a concept

A cozy reward for writing (owner, 14 October). Nothing here is built yet: it is the concept to decide on, and
the brief for the art. When it is decided, it becomes requirements (AT-10 and on) in `requirements.md`.

## The idea

**Something grows out of the Pensieve basin as Eva writes.** The basin is already the app's mark, and in the
Forest look a sprout already rises from it. In the garden, a seed in the basin becomes a sprout, a sapling
and, over a long book, a tree in blossom. Around it, each thing she does well leaves a small **keepsake**:
flowers for her words, little books for her chapters, birds for her notes, butterflies for her ideas, a teacup
for each run of pomodoros.

The Goals panel beside the page becomes the garden. It keeps her goals (today, this chapter) under the
picture, and gets a cuter name: **Vrtiček** in Slovenian, **Garden** in English.

## What keeps it cozy

The app's own rules come first. The owner dropped streaks on 6 October, and the garden keeps that spirit.

- **Nothing is ever lost.** No streaks, no "days in a row", no wilting. A day off takes nothing away, and
  deleting words or notes never takes a keepsake back.
- **Never pushy.** No reminders, no "you haven't written today", no red dots that won't go away. The garden
  waits in its panel until she looks.
- **Never in the way.** Nothing appears while she types. A new keepsake is told quietly when she pauses or
  leaves the page; in Zen, only after Zen. In Gentle mode nothing moves.
- **Honest.** Words count as Pensieve already counts "words today": typing and pasting do; a duplicated
  chapter, a restored version or a brought-in Word file don't.
- **Never slower.** The garden is worked out when Pensieve saves, never on a key. The picture is a few still
  images laid on each other.

The Goals panel is 300 px wide beside the page. The garden sits on top, her goals stay below:

```
│                                   │
│ NASLEDNJI SPOMINKI                │
│ ✿ Deset tisoč      7.420 / 10.000 │
│ ✦ Deset zapiskov          6 / 10  │
│                                   │

│ TO POGLAVJE    1.240 od 2.000 ▓▓▓ │
│                                   │
│ Vsi spominki →                    │
└───────────────────────────────────┘
```

- **The picture**: about 270 × 180 px, the basin at the bottom, the plant, and every keepsake earned so far.
  A keepsake not yet earned isn't drawn at all, so the garden looks finished at every stage, just smaller.
- **The stage** and how far to the next one.
- **Next keepsakes**: the two or three nearest, with how far she is. Never a list of everything missing.
- **Her goals**, as now.
- **All keepsakes** opens a window: every keepsake as a small picture with its name and the day it came;
  those not yet earned are soft outlines with a line on how they come ("10 notes"). Rituals stay "?".

The toolbar's Goals button becomes Garden (Vrtiček), with today's words as now.

## When a keepsake comes

- A small card above the floating bar, for a few seconds: the keepsake's picture and "Nov spominek v vrtičku:
  Tisoč besed". It comes when she pauses (a few seconds without typing) or leaves the page, never mid-sentence.
  In Zen it waits until she leaves Zen.
- The Garden button shows a soft dot until she opens the panel, where the new keepsake fades in.
- A new stage is a slightly bigger moment: the picture changes, with a line ("Vaš vrtiček je zdaj
  Drevesce").
- A soft chime, only when the ambience or the keys' sounds are on.
- Gentle mode: the card and the dot, no fading and no sound.

## Counting

- **One garden for her**, for all projects together, like "words today", which is already counted across
  projects. It lives in her settings (the prefs file), not in a project, so it survives moving or deleting
  projects.
- **Words written** = the words of each day before, added up as each day ends, plus today's. Pensieve keeps
  only today's count now, so the garden adds a running total.
- **The starting garden.** When the garden first opens, the words already in her own projects count once
  (not the example books or the long test book), and the keepsakes they earn come quietly, with one line: "Vaš
  vrtiček je zrasel iz tega, kar ste že napisali." Pomodoros, Zen and the rituals start from nothing; there is
  no record of them before.
- **Counts never go down.** For chapters, notes, ideas and to-dos the garden keeps the highest number it has
  seen, so deleting a note never takes a bird away.
- **Not counted:** words in documents brought in from Word (that is the "Rokopis je doma" keepsake instead),
  duplicated or restored text, the example books.

## The art brief

### Style

The same as the app's art now: **flat vector, simple geometric shapes, soft gradients at most, no outlines,
no text, no faces with detail**. Warm, quiet, a little naive, like the Basin mark, the empty-state pictures
and the Zen scenes (`static/art/`). SVG if the tool can make it (as all the app's art is), else PNG with a
transparent background at twice the size.

Colours from the mark and the looks:

| What | Colour |
|---|---|
| Plant, deep green | #3f7d4e |
| Plant, light green (the Forest sprout) | #74b350 |
| Terracotta (Daylight's basin, books, flowers) | #a94f2e |
| Sun orange (flowers, fruit, lantern light) | #e0975a |
| Warm flame (fireflies, lantern) | #e9a265, core #f6d2a8 |
| Lavender (Moonlit, butterflies) | #a99cf5 |
| Paper cream | #fcf9f2 |

### One canvas for every piece

Every file is **600 × 400** (3:2), drawn so the pieces lie exactly on each other:

- **The basin** sits in the middle at the bottom: centred at x 300, its rim at about y 300, about 160 wide,
  the same in every file.
- **The plant** grows up from the basin's middle; at its biggest its crown fits between x 150 and 450 and
  stays below y 20.
- **The ground** is a soft band from y 300 down; ground keepsakes stand on it to the left (x 30 to 240) and
  right (x 360 to 570) of the basin.
- **The air** above the ground and beside the plant (y 40 to 220) is for birds, butterflies and fireflies.
- **Each keepsake is its own file on the full canvas**, drawn in its final place, the rest transparent. The
  app simply lays the files on each other: no positions to work out. Easiest is to draw the whole finished
  garden once, then save each element as its own layer file.

### The files

| Files | What | How many |
|---|---|---|
| `backdrop-{daylight,sunset,candlelit,moonlit,forest}` | The look's sky or halo, its ground and the basin, in the look's colours (the basin as the mark has it) | 5 |
| `stage-1` … `stage-8` | The plant alone, from a seed in the basin to the story tree, in the same greens for every look | 8 |
| `flower-1` … `flower-9` | Word milestones: small flowers on the ground, a little bigger as they go; the last three may be fruit on the tree (stages 6 to 8 only) | 9 |
| `firefly-1` … `firefly-5` | Writing days: small glowing dots in the air | 5 |
| `butterfly-1` … `butterfly-6` | Good days and ideas | 6 |
| `can`, `bee-1`, `bee-2` | The daily goal | 3 |
| `books-1` … `books-3`, `ribbon`, `manuscript` | Chapters (books stacked by the basin, the pile growing), a book's target, the brought-in manuscript | 5 |
| `bird-1` … `bird-3` | Notes | 3 |
| `stone-1` … `stone-3` | To-dos: stepping stones towards the basin | 3 |
| `cup-1` … `cup-3`, `lantern`, `chime` | Pomodoros and Zen | 5 |
| `robin`, `owl`, `rainbow`, `gate` | The rituals | 4 |

About 56 small files. The keepsake files double as the pictures in the All keepsakes window, cut from the
canvas.

The plant and keepsakes are the same for every look, so they must read on both a light and a dark backdrop:
mid tones, with a lighter edge where light falls. The fireflies and the lantern should glow (a soft halo in the
file), which shows best in Candlelit and Moonlit.

### A prompt to start from

> Flat vector illustration, simple geometric shapes, soft warm palette (deep green #3f7d4e, light green
> #74b350, terracotta #a94f2e, warm orange #e0975a, cream #fcf9f2), no outlines, no text, transparent
> background, 600 × 400. A small stone basin at the bottom centre (the Pensieve logo's basin), with
> [stage: a young sapling with a few leaves] growing out of it. Cozy, quiet, naive, like a children's book
> printed in few colours.

Then, per keepsake: "the same scene, only [three small stacked books, muted colours] on the ground to the
right of the basin, everything else transparent".

## For the owner to decide

1. **The name:** Vrtiček / Garden? (Or Drevo zgodb / Story tree, or Gaj / Grove.)
2. **What grows:** one tree for every look, as above? (Or what rises from the basin in each look grows:
   the sun, the flame, the moon, the sprout; prettier, but four kinds of art.)
3. **The starting garden:** do the words already in her projects count once, so the garden starts grown?
4. **Brought-in manuscripts:** a keepsake of their own (as above), or do their words count too?
5. **Sound:** a soft chime when the sounds are on, or never?
6. **Home:** leave the garden in its panel, or also show the little plant on Home later?
7. **The list:** too many keepsakes, too few, any to drop or add?
